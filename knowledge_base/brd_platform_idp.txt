# Business Requirements Document
# Project: Internal Developer Platform (IDP) - Self-Service Infrastructure
# Domain: Platform Engineering | Complexity: Complex | Version: 2.0

## Background and Business Context
Engineering teams at the organization spend an average of 3.2 days per sprint on
infrastructure provisioning, environment setup, and deployment pipeline maintenance.
This represents 32% of total engineering capacity lost to toil. The organization
has 14 product teams, 180 engineers, and 340 microservices deployed across three
cloud regions. The goal is an Internal Developer Platform that abstracts infrastructure
complexity and enables teams to self-serve environment provisioning, deployments,
and observability in under 10 minutes without requiring platform team involvement.

## Objectives
1. Reduce environment provisioning time from 3.2 days to under 10 minutes
2. Enable 100% of product teams to deploy independently without platform team tickets
3. Standardize observability across all 340 microservices
4. Reduce cloud infrastructure costs by 20% through rightsizing and waste elimination
5. Achieve SOC 2 Type II compliance for the platform layer

## Stakeholders
- Sponsor: CTO
- Platform Team: 8 engineers (owners and builders of the IDP)
- Product Teams: 14 teams (primary users)
- Security: InfoSec team (approvers for compliance requirements)
- Finance: FinOps team (cost visibility requirements)

## Functional Requirements
FR-01: Engineers shall provision new environments via a web UI or CLI in under 10 minutes
FR-02: The platform shall support templated service blueprints (Node.js, Python, Java, Go)
FR-03: The platform shall provide automated CI/CD pipeline generation per service blueprint
FR-04: The platform shall enforce resource quotas per team to prevent runaway costs
FR-05: The platform shall provide a unified observability dashboard (logs, metrics, traces)
FR-06: The platform shall support canary deployments and automated rollback on error threshold
FR-07: The platform shall generate cost attribution reports per team per sprint
FR-08: The platform shall integrate with existing GitHub Enterprise for source control
FR-09: The platform shall support secrets management via HashiCorp Vault integration
FR-10: The platform shall provide a service catalog showing all 340 services with ownership
FR-11: The platform shall send automated alerts when services breach SLO thresholds
FR-12: The platform shall support multi-region deployments (us-east-1, eu-west-1, ap-south-1)

## Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)
NFR-01 Performance: Platform API response time < 500ms at P99
NFR-02 Availability: 99.9% uptime - platform downtime blocks all product deployments
NFR-03 Scalability: Support 1,000 concurrent deployment jobs without queue degradation
NFR-04 Security: Zero-trust networking, RBAC per team, all secrets encrypted at rest
NFR-05 Auditability: Full audit trail for all infrastructure changes with 2-year retention
NFR-06 Extensibility: Plugin architecture allowing teams to add custom deployment targets

## Constraints
- Must work with existing GitHub Enterprise - no migration to GitLab
- Kubernetes on EKS is the only approved container runtime
- HashiCorp Vault already deployed - secrets management cannot change
- Platform team budget: $1.2M per year including infrastructure
- Must not require product teams to learn new programming languages
- Rollout must be incremental - cannot force all 14 teams to migrate simultaneously

## Risks
- RISK-01 (HIGH): 14 teams have divergent toolchains - standardization will cause friction
- RISK-02 (HIGH): Platform becomes single point of failure for all product deployments
- RISK-03 (MEDIUM): Kubernetes expertise gap in 6 of 14 product teams
- RISK-04 (MEDIUM): Cost attribution requires instrumentation changes in existing services
- RISK-05 (LOW): HashiCorp Vault licensing costs may increase with usage growth

## Assumptions
- All product teams will migrate to IDP within 12 months of launch
- Platform team maintains 24/7 on-call rotation for platform incidents
- Existing Jenkins pipelines will be replaced, not integrated

## Success Criteria
- 10 of 14 teams fully migrated and self-serving within 6 months of launch
- Zero platform-related deployment incidents in first 90 days post-migration
- Mean time to provision new environment < 8 minutes (measured over 30 days)
- 20% reduction in cloud spend within 9 months of full adoption
