# Business Requirements Document
# Project: Customer Payment Portal Modernization
# Domain: Fintech | Complexity: Medium | Version: 1.2

## Background and Business Context
The existing payment portal was built in 2019 and relies on a monolithic architecture
that cannot support the organization's 40% year-over-year transaction growth. Customer
complaints about checkout latency have increased 28% in the past two quarters. The
business requires a modernized payment portal that scales horizontally and supports
new payment methods including digital wallets and BNPL (Buy Now Pay Later).

## Objectives
1. Reduce average checkout latency from 4.2 seconds to under 1.5 seconds
2. Support 10,000 concurrent users during peak periods (Black Friday, end of month)
3. Integrate three new payment methods: Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna BNPL
4. Achieve PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance certification within 6 months of launch
5. Reduce payment failure rate from 3.1% to under 0.8%

## Stakeholders
- Product Owner: VP of Digital Commerce
- Technical Lead: Principal Engineer, Payments Platform
- Compliance: Chief Information Security Officer
- Business: Head of Revenue Operations
- External: Payment gateway vendor (Stripe), PCI-DSS auditor

## Functional Requirements
FR-01: The system shall process credit card, debit card, and digital wallet payments
FR-02: The system shall support 3D Secure 2.0 authentication for card transactions
FR-03: The system shall provide real-time payment status webhooks to merchant systems
FR-04: The system shall store payment method tokens (never raw card data) per PCI-DSS
FR-05: The system shall support multi-currency transactions in 12 currencies
FR-06: The system shall provide a merchant dashboard with transaction analytics
FR-07: The system shall support full and partial refunds within 180 days of transaction
FR-08: The system shall integrate with existing ERP system via REST API
FR-09: The system shall send payment confirmation emails within 30 seconds of completion
FR-10: The system shall support subscription billing with configurable billing cycles

## Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)
NFR-01 Performance: P95 checkout latency < 1.5 seconds under normal load
NFR-02 Availability: 99.95% uptime SLA (maximum 4.38 hours downtime per year)
NFR-03 Scalability: Auto-scale from 100 to 10,000 concurrent users within 90 seconds
NFR-04 Security: PCI-DSS Level 1, SOC 2 Type II, end-to-end encryption in transit and at rest
NFR-05 Recoverability: RPO < 1 hour, RTO < 4 hours for any regional failure
NFR-06 Compliance: GDPR data residency - EU customer data must remain in EU regions
NFR-07 Observability: All transactions must be traceable end-to-end within 5 minutes of incident

## Constraints
- Must integrate with existing Salesforce CRM - no replacement
- Legacy Oracle database must remain as system of record for 18 months (migration later)
- Engineering team: 6 engineers, 1 QA, 1 DevOps - no additional hiring approved
- Budget: $480,000 total including infrastructure for first year
- Timeline: MVP in 4 months, full feature set in 8 months
- Must use AWS (existing enterprise agreement with volume discounts)

## Risks
- RISK-01 (HIGH): PCI-DSS audit timeline may slip if third-party auditor availability is limited
- RISK-02 (MEDIUM): Stripe API migration from v2 to v3 requires full regression testing
- RISK-03 (MEDIUM): Legacy Oracle integration may surface undocumented data schemas
- RISK-04 (LOW): Team lacks BNPL integration experience - learning curve expected

## Success Criteria
- Payment success rate >= 99.2% in production after 30 days
- Zero PCI-DSS findings in external audit
- Customer satisfaction score (CSAT) for checkout >= 4.2/5.0
- All NFRs validated by load testing before go-live
