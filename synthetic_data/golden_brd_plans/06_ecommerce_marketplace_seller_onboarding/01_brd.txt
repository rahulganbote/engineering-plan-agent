# Business Requirements Document
# Project: Marketplace Seller Onboarding and Compliance
# Domain: Ecommerce | Synthetic Golden Dataset | Version: 1.0

## Background and Business Context
Automate seller onboarding, document checks, tax validation, and catalog readiness for a curated marketplace.

Current state: Seller onboarding takes 18 days on average due to manual document review and repeated catalog-quality rework.

Primary users: Marketplace sellers, compliance analysts, category managers.

## Objectives
1. Reduce median seller onboarding time from 18 days to 5 days.
2. Auto-approve 70% of low-risk sellers after KYC and tax checks.
3. Catch restricted-product catalog issues before first listing publish.
4. Maintain a complete audit history for seller decisions.

## Stakeholders
- Product Owner: Seller Experience Product Lead
- Technical Lead: Senior Engineering Manager, Marketplace
- Business Sponsor: GM Marketplace
- Primary Users: Marketplace sellers, compliance analysts, category managers
- Compliance / Security: KYC/KYB, sanctions screening, marketplace product policy, SOC 2 control owners
- External: Vendors and platform teams for Identity verification vendor, tax validation API, sanctions screening API, catalog service, seller portal, payout platform

## Scope Boundaries
In Scope:
- Seller application workflow with document upload.
- KYC, tax ID, sanctions, and bank-account validation integrations.
- Catalog readiness checks for required attributes and restricted categories.
- Compliance review queue with decision evidence.

Out of Scope:
- Seller advertising tools.
- Payout disbursement engine changes.
- Cross-border VAT filing.

## Functional Requirements
FR-01: The system shall provide Seller application workflow with document upload.
FR-02: The system shall integrate with Identity verification vendor, tax validation API, sanctions screening API, catalog service, seller portal, payout platform.
FR-03: The system shall manage data assets including Seller profiles, business documents, tax IDs, bank verification status, catalog submissions, review decisions.
FR-04: The system shall enforce compliance controls for KYC/KYB, sanctions screening, marketplace product policy, SOC 2.
FR-05: The system shall expose role-based workflows for primary users.
FR-06: The system shall record decision provenance, user actions, and system-generated outputs.
FR-07: The system shall provide operational dashboards for throughput, failures, and business KPIs.
FR-08: The system shall support export or downstream handoff for approved outputs.
FR-09: The system shall handle partial integration failures with retry, queueing, and user-visible status.
FR-10: The system shall support audit review by exporting evidence for selected records.

## Non-Functional Requirements
NFR-01 Performance: Seller dashboard actions respond in <= 2 seconds P95.
NFR-02 Availability: 99.9% uptime for seller onboarding workflows.
NFR-03 Scalability: Support 10,000 concurrent seller applications during campaigns.
NFR-04 Security: Encrypt business documents and restrict analyst access by case assignment.
NFR-05 Recoverability: RPO <= 15 minutes and RTO <= 1 hour.
NFR-06 Auditability: Every automated and manual decision must include evidence and policy version.

## Constraints
- Team: Cross-functional product squad with 5-8 engineers, QA, security, and part-time domain experts.
- Timeline: MVP target in 14 weeks; production launch target in 18 weeks.
- Budget: Implementation must fit an annualized run-rate approved by the business sponsor.
- Technology: Prefer organization-approved cloud, identity, observability, and RAG tooling.
- Compliance: KYC/KYB, sanctions screening, marketplace product policy, SOC 2 requirements must be traceable before launch.

## Risks
- RISK-01 (HIGH): KYC vendor false negatives can block legitimate sellers - Mitigation: Route borderline results to manual review and monitor appeal outcomes.
- RISK-02 (HIGH): Restricted product rules may be incomplete - Mitigation: Category manager review and versioned policy tests.
- RISK-03 (MEDIUM): Document upload PII increases breach impact - Mitigation: Use short-lived URLs and malware scanning.

## Acceptance Criteria
- FR-01 through FR-04 pass integration tests using representative synthetic and historical sample data.
- All NFRs have automated or operational validation evidence before go-live.
- Security review confirms encryption, access control, audit logging, and data-retention behavior.
- Business owner signs off on pilot KPI dashboard and exception workflow.

## Success Criteria Post Launch
- Reduce median seller onboarding time from 18 days to 5 days.
- Auto-approve 70% of low-risk sellers after KYC and tax checks.
- Catch restricted-product catalog issues before first listing publish.
