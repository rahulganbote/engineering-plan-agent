# Business Requirements Document
# Project: Provider Payment Reconciliation Hub
# Domain: Insurance Payments | Synthetic Golden Dataset | Version: 1.0

## Background and Business Context
Automate reconciliation between claim payments, bank disbursements, and provider remittance advice.

Current state: Manual reconciliation of provider payments creates month-end delays and unresolved exceptions over $4M per quarter.

Primary users: Payment operations analysts, provider support, finance controllers.

## Objectives
1. Auto-match 92% of payment lines across claims, bank files, and remittance advice.
2. Reduce month-end reconciliation cycle from 5 business days to 1 business day.
3. Create exception queues with root-cause classification.
4. Provide SOX-ready audit evidence for every adjustment.

## Stakeholders
- Product Owner: Payment Operations Product Lead
- Technical Lead: Staff Engineer, Payments
- Business Sponsor: CFO, Health Insurance Operations
- Primary Users: Payment operations analysts, provider support, finance controllers
- Compliance / Security: HIPAA, SOX, SOC 2, NACHA file handling control owners
- External: Vendors and platform teams for Claims payment platform, bank SFTP, EDI 835 parser, ERP general ledger, identity provider

## Scope Boundaries
In Scope:
- Daily ingestion of ACH files, claims payment extracts, and ERA/835 remittance files.
- Deterministic and fuzzy matching rules.
- Exception workflow with analyst notes and approvals.
- Finance export for resolved adjustments.

Out of Scope:
- Changing claim adjudication logic.
- Provider portal redesign.
- Real-time bank settlement.

## Functional Requirements
FR-01: The system shall provide Daily ingestion of ACH files, claims payment extracts, and ERA/835 remittance files.
FR-02: The system shall integrate with Claims payment platform, bank SFTP, EDI 835 parser, ERP general ledger, identity provider.
FR-03: The system shall manage data assets including Payment extracts, ACH files, EDI 835 remittances, provider master data, GL adjustment records.
FR-04: The system shall enforce compliance controls for HIPAA, SOX, SOC 2, NACHA file handling.
FR-05: The system shall expose role-based workflows for primary users.
FR-06: The system shall record decision provenance, user actions, and system-generated outputs.
FR-07: The system shall provide operational dashboards for throughput, failures, and business KPIs.
FR-08: The system shall support export or downstream handoff for approved outputs.
FR-09: The system shall handle partial integration failures with retry, queueing, and user-visible status.
FR-10: The system shall support audit review by exporting evidence for selected records.

## Non-Functional Requirements
NFR-01 Performance: Process 1 million payment lines in <= 45 minutes.
NFR-02 Availability: 99.5% uptime with batch restartability.
NFR-03 Scalability: Support 2x annual enrollment peak volume.
NFR-04 Security: Encrypt PHI and payment data; mask bank account details in UI.
NFR-05 Recoverability: RPO <= 30 minutes and restart failed batch from last checkpoint.
NFR-06 Auditability: Retain reconciliation evidence and approvals for 7 years.

## Constraints
- Team: Cross-functional product squad with 5-8 engineers, QA, security, and part-time domain experts.
- Timeline: MVP target in 13 weeks; production launch target in 17 weeks.
- Budget: Implementation must fit an annualized run-rate approved by the business sponsor.
- Technology: Prefer organization-approved cloud, identity, observability, and RAG tooling.
- Compliance: HIPAA, SOX, SOC 2, NACHA file handling requirements must be traceable before launch.

## Risks
- RISK-01 (HIGH): EDI file quality inconsistencies can reduce match rate - Mitigation: Profile historical files and create exception taxonomy early.
- RISK-02 (MEDIUM): Bank file delivery windows may slip - Mitigation: Add SLA monitoring and late-file alerting.
- RISK-03 (MEDIUM): SOX control evidence may be insufficient - Mitigation: Review workflow with finance control owners in Sprint 1.

## Acceptance Criteria
- FR-01 through FR-04 pass integration tests using representative synthetic and historical sample data.
- All NFRs have automated or operational validation evidence before go-live.
- Security review confirms encryption, access control, audit logging, and data-retention behavior.
- Business owner signs off on pilot KPI dashboard and exception workflow.

## Success Criteria Post Launch
- Auto-match 92% of payment lines across claims, bank files, and remittance advice.
- Reduce month-end reconciliation cycle from 5 business days to 1 business day.
- Create exception queues with root-cause classification.
