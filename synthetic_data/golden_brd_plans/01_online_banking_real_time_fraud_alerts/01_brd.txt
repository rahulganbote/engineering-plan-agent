# Business Requirements Document
# Project: Real-Time Digital Banking Fraud Alerts
# Domain: Online Banking | Synthetic Golden Dataset | Version: 1.0

## Background and Business Context
Detect risky digital banking activity and deliver customer-facing alerts before fraudulent transfers settle.

Current state: ACH and instant-payment fraud losses increased 18% quarter over quarter, while analyst review queues miss the two-minute alert window.

Primary users: Retail banking customers, fraud analysts, contact-center agents.

## Objectives
1. Reduce confirmed unauthorized digital transfer losses by 25% within two quarters.
2. Notify customers of high-risk activity within 30 seconds at P95.
3. Give analysts an explainable risk timeline with event provenance for every alert.
4. Preserve FFIEC-aligned audit trails for all risk decisions and overrides.

## Stakeholders
- Product Owner: Product Manager, Fraud Operations
- Technical Lead: Engineering Lead, Digital Channels
- Business Sponsor: VP Digital Banking
- Primary Users: Retail banking customers, fraud analysts, contact-center agents
- Compliance / Security: FFIEC, GLBA, SOC 2, internal model-risk review control owners
- External: Vendors and platform teams for Core banking API, identity provider, event stream, SMS gateway, mobile push service, case-management system

## Scope Boundaries
In Scope:
- Event ingestion from online banking login, payee, ACH, wire, and Zelle-like transfer streams.
- Risk scoring API with rules plus ML model feature lookup.
- Customer notification through mobile push, SMS fallback, and secure inbox.
- Analyst console API for alert disposition and false-positive tracking.

Out of Scope:
- Core banking ledger changes.
- New customer authentication factors beyond existing MFA integrations.
- International payment rails.

## Functional Requirements
FR-01: The system shall provide Event ingestion from online banking login, payee, ACH, wire, and Zelle-like transfer streams.
FR-02: The system shall integrate with Core banking API, identity provider, event stream, SMS gateway, mobile push service, case-management system.
FR-03: The system shall manage data assets including Digital session events, transfer metadata, device intelligence, customer contact preferences, analyst dispositions.
FR-04: The system shall enforce compliance controls for FFIEC, GLBA, SOC 2, internal model-risk review.
FR-05: The system shall expose role-based workflows for primary users.
FR-06: The system shall record decision provenance, user actions, and system-generated outputs.
FR-07: The system shall provide operational dashboards for throughput, failures, and business KPIs.
FR-08: The system shall support export or downstream handoff for approved outputs.
FR-09: The system shall handle partial integration failures with retry, queueing, and user-visible status.
FR-10: The system shall support audit review by exporting evidence for selected records.

## Non-Functional Requirements
NFR-01 Performance: P95 risk decision latency <= 700 ms for 150 events per second.
NFR-02 Availability: 99.95% monthly uptime for the scoring and notification path.
NFR-03 Scalability: Scale to 3x Cyber Monday traffic without manual infrastructure changes.
NFR-04 Security: Encrypt PII at rest and in transit; enforce least-privilege analyst access.
NFR-05 Recoverability: RPO <= 5 minutes and RTO <= 30 minutes for alerting services.
NFR-06 Observability: Trace every alert from event ingest to customer notification within 5 minutes.

## Constraints
- Team: Cross-functional product squad with 5-8 engineers, QA, security, and part-time domain experts.
- Timeline: MVP target in 14 weeks; production launch target in 18 weeks.
- Budget: Implementation must fit an annualized run-rate approved by the business sponsor.
- Technology: Prefer organization-approved cloud, identity, observability, and RAG tooling.
- Compliance: FFIEC, GLBA, SOC 2, internal model-risk review requirements must be traceable before launch.

## Risks
- RISK-01 (HIGH): False positives may erode customer trust - Mitigation: Tune thresholds with analyst feedback and customer friction metrics.
- RISK-02 (HIGH): Payment stream schema changes can break scoring - Mitigation: Use contract tests and schema registry gates.
- RISK-03 (MEDIUM): SMS provider throttling may delay alerts - Mitigation: Use push-first routing and multi-provider failover.

## Acceptance Criteria
- FR-01 through FR-04 pass integration tests using representative synthetic and historical sample data.
- All NFRs have automated or operational validation evidence before go-live.
- Security review confirms encryption, access control, audit logging, and data-retention behavior.
- Business owner signs off on pilot KPI dashboard and exception workflow.

## Success Criteria Post Launch
- Reduce confirmed unauthorized digital transfer losses by 25% within two quarters.
- Notify customers of high-risk activity within 30 seconds at P95.
- Give analysts an explainable risk timeline with event provenance for every alert.
