# Business Requirements Document
# Project: Contract Clause Intelligence and Playbook Review
# Domain: Legal Technology | Synthetic Golden Dataset | Version: 1.0

## Background and Business Context
Assist legal teams by extracting clauses, comparing them to approved playbooks, and producing review notes with citations.

Current state: Legal review queues delay sales cycles because routine contracts require manual clause comparison against playbooks.

Primary users: Commercial counsel, contract managers, sales operations.

## Objectives
1. Reduce first-pass contract review time from 90 minutes to 25 minutes.
2. Classify standard clauses with >= 88% precision.
3. Generate citation-backed deviations from approved fallback language.
4. Preserve attorney control over final recommendations.

## Stakeholders
- Product Owner: Legal Operations Product Manager
- Technical Lead: Staff Engineer, Legal Tech
- Business Sponsor: General Counsel
- Primary Users: Commercial counsel, contract managers, sales operations
- Compliance / Security: Attorney-client privilege controls, SOC 2, GDPR for EU contracts control owners
- External: Vendors and platform teams for Document management system, legal playbook repository, CRM opportunity metadata, identity provider

## Scope Boundaries
In Scope:
- Upload and parse DOCX/PDF contracts.
- Clause extraction for indemnity, limitation of liability, privacy, termination, and payment terms.
- RAG comparison against approved legal playbooks.
- Review memo export with citations and confidence scores.

Out of Scope:
- Autonomous contract negotiation.
- E-signature workflow replacement.
- Legal advice to non-attorney users without review.

## Functional Requirements
FR-01: The system shall provide Upload and parse DOCX/PDF contracts.
FR-02: The system shall integrate with Document management system, legal playbook repository, CRM opportunity metadata, identity provider.
FR-03: The system shall manage data assets including Contracts, clause library, legal playbooks, fallback terms, opportunity metadata, reviewer notes.
FR-04: The system shall enforce compliance controls for Attorney-client privilege controls, SOC 2, GDPR for EU contracts.
FR-05: The system shall expose role-based workflows for primary users.
FR-06: The system shall record decision provenance, user actions, and system-generated outputs.
FR-07: The system shall provide operational dashboards for throughput, failures, and business KPIs.
FR-08: The system shall support export or downstream handoff for approved outputs.
FR-09: The system shall handle partial integration failures with retry, queueing, and user-visible status.
FR-10: The system shall support audit review by exporting evidence for selected records.

## Non-Functional Requirements
NFR-01 Performance: Analyze a 60-page contract in <= 3 minutes P95.
NFR-02 Availability: 99.5% uptime for legal review workbench.
NFR-03 Scalability: Support 2,000 contract analyses per month.
NFR-04 Security: Encrypt documents and restrict matter access by legal team membership.
NFR-05 Recoverability: RPO <= 15 minutes and RTO <= 2 hours.
NFR-06 Explainability: Every recommendation must cite source clause and playbook passage.

## Constraints
- Team: Cross-functional product squad with 5-8 engineers, QA, security, and part-time domain experts.
- Timeline: MVP target in 12 weeks; production launch target in 16 weeks.
- Budget: Implementation must fit an annualized run-rate approved by the business sponsor.
- Technology: Prefer organization-approved cloud, identity, observability, and RAG tooling.
- Compliance: Attorney-client privilege controls, SOC 2, GDPR for EU contracts requirements must be traceable before launch.

## Risks
- RISK-01 (HIGH): Hallucinated legal guidance could create liability - Mitigation: Force citation-backed outputs and attorney approval before sharing.
- RISK-02 (HIGH): Privilege leakage through model provider is unacceptable - Mitigation: Use approved enterprise model endpoint with no training retention.
- RISK-03 (MEDIUM): PDF extraction quality varies - Mitigation: Benchmark parser confidence and route low-confidence docs to manual review.

## Acceptance Criteria
- FR-01 through FR-04 pass integration tests using representative synthetic and historical sample data.
- All NFRs have automated or operational validation evidence before go-live.
- Security review confirms encryption, access control, audit logging, and data-retention behavior.
- Business owner signs off on pilot KPI dashboard and exception workflow.

## Success Criteria Post Launch
- Reduce first-pass contract review time from 90 minutes to 25 minutes.
- Classify standard clauses with >= 88% precision.
- Generate citation-backed deviations from approved fallback language.
