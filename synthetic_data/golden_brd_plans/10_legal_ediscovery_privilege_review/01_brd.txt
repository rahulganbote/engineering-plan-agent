# Business Requirements Document
# Project: eDiscovery Privilege Review Prioritization
# Domain: Legal Technology | Synthetic Golden Dataset | Version: 1.0

## Background and Business Context
Prioritize potentially privileged documents for attorney review using metadata, communication graph signals, and explainable classification.

Current state: Large matters require expensive linear review, and privileged documents risk accidental production when reviewer queues are overloaded.

Primary users: Litigation counsel, eDiscovery analysts, outside counsel reviewers.

## Objectives
1. Prioritize top 20% of documents to capture >= 75% of privileged items.
2. Reduce outside counsel first-pass review spend by 30%.
3. Explain privilege signals without exposing unnecessary sensitive content.
4. Maintain chain-of-custody and defensible audit records.

## Stakeholders
- Product Owner: eDiscovery Program Manager
- Technical Lead: Engineering Manager, Legal Data
- Business Sponsor: Deputy General Counsel, Litigation
- Primary Users: Litigation counsel, eDiscovery analysts, outside counsel reviewers
- Compliance / Security: Attorney-client privilege, legal hold chain-of-custody, SOC 2, GDPR where applicable control owners
- External: Vendors and platform teams for Relativity or equivalent eDiscovery API, identity provider, matter-management system, secure audit store

## Scope Boundaries
In Scope:
- Ingest review-set metadata and extracted text from eDiscovery platform.
- Classify privilege likelihood using attorney/domain lists, keywords, and graph features.
- Generate prioritized review batches with explanation codes.
- Export reviewer decisions back to eDiscovery platform.

Out of Scope:
- Final privilege determination without attorney review.
- Native file processing and OCR pipeline.
- Legal hold collection tools.

## Functional Requirements
FR-01: The system shall provide Ingest review-set metadata and extracted text from eDiscovery platform.
FR-02: The system shall integrate with Relativity or equivalent eDiscovery API, identity provider, matter-management system, secure audit store.
FR-03: The system shall manage data assets including Email metadata, extracted text, custodian lists, attorney lists, reviewer decisions, matter metadata.
FR-04: The system shall enforce compliance controls for Attorney-client privilege, legal hold chain-of-custody, SOC 2, GDPR where applicable.
FR-05: The system shall expose role-based workflows for primary users.
FR-06: The system shall record decision provenance, user actions, and system-generated outputs.
FR-07: The system shall provide operational dashboards for throughput, failures, and business KPIs.
FR-08: The system shall support export or downstream handoff for approved outputs.
FR-09: The system shall handle partial integration failures with retry, queueing, and user-visible status.
FR-10: The system shall support audit review by exporting evidence for selected records.

## Non-Functional Requirements
NFR-01 Performance: Score 1 million documents in <= 6 hours.
NFR-02 Availability: 99.5% uptime for batch management and exports.
NFR-03 Scalability: Support matters up to 25 million documents using partitioned batch processing.
NFR-04 Security: Matter-level isolation and encrypted processing workspaces.
NFR-05 Recoverability: RPO <= 30 minutes and rerunnable scoring batches.
NFR-06 Defensibility: Retain model version, features, and reviewer action logs for every score.

## Constraints
- Team: Cross-functional product squad with 5-8 engineers, QA, security, and part-time domain experts.
- Timeline: MVP target in 16 weeks; production launch target in 20 weeks.
- Budget: Implementation must fit an annualized run-rate approved by the business sponsor.
- Technology: Prefer organization-approved cloud, identity, observability, and RAG tooling.
- Compliance: Attorney-client privilege, legal hold chain-of-custody, SOC 2, GDPR where applicable requirements must be traceable before launch.

## Risks
- RISK-01 (HIGH): False negatives could lead to privilege waiver - Mitigation: Use conservative thresholds and mandatory attorney review for low-confidence sets.
- RISK-02 (HIGH): Matter data leakage across workspaces is unacceptable - Mitigation: Enforce strict tenant isolation and automated access tests.
- RISK-03 (MEDIUM): Opposing counsel may challenge model defensibility - Mitigation: Log features, versions, thresholds, and validation reports.

## Acceptance Criteria
- FR-01 through FR-04 pass integration tests using representative synthetic and historical sample data.
- All NFRs have automated or operational validation evidence before go-live.
- Security review confirms encryption, access control, audit logging, and data-retention behavior.
- Business owner signs off on pilot KPI dashboard and exception workflow.

## Success Criteria Post Launch
- Prioritize top 20% of documents to capture >= 75% of privileged items.
- Reduce outside counsel first-pass review spend by 30%.
- Explain privilege signals without exposing unnecessary sensitive content.
