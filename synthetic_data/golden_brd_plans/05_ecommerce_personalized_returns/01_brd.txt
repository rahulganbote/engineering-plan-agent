# Business Requirements Document
# Project: Personalized Returns and Exchange Experience
# Domain: Ecommerce | Synthetic Golden Dataset | Version: 1.0

## Background and Business Context
Reduce return friction and preserve revenue by recommending exchanges, store credit, or guided return paths based on order context.

Current state: Return rate rose to 21% and customer-service contacts increased during peak season, while exchange opportunities are missed.

Primary users: Shoppers, customer-service agents, warehouse returns team.

## Objectives
1. Increase exchange selection from 12% to 22% of eligible returns.
2. Reduce return-related support contacts by 30%.
3. Issue return labels within 10 seconds for eligible orders.
4. Provide warehouse-ready disposition data before package arrival.

## Stakeholders
- Product Owner: Product Manager, Post-Purchase
- Technical Lead: Engineering Lead, Commerce Platform
- Business Sponsor: VP Customer Experience
- Primary Users: Shoppers, customer-service agents, warehouse returns team
- Compliance / Security: PCI scope avoidance, consumer refund laws, SOC 2 control owners
- External: Vendors and platform teams for Commerce order API, PIM, inventory API, carrier label API, WMS, customer-service CRM

## Scope Boundaries
In Scope:
- Self-service return initiation from order history.
- Eligibility rules for refunds, exchanges, warranties, and final-sale exclusions.
- Recommendation service for size/color exchanges and store credit incentives.
- Carrier label generation and warehouse pre-advice events.

Out of Scope:
- Marketplace seller dispute workflow.
- International tax refund automation.
- New warehouse management system implementation.

## Functional Requirements
FR-01: The system shall provide Self-service return initiation from order history.
FR-02: The system shall integrate with Commerce order API, PIM, inventory API, carrier label API, WMS, customer-service CRM.
FR-03: The system shall manage data assets including Orders, product attributes, inventory, return reasons, customer preferences, carrier labels.
FR-04: The system shall enforce compliance controls for PCI scope avoidance, consumer refund laws, SOC 2.
FR-05: The system shall expose role-based workflows for primary users.
FR-06: The system shall record decision provenance, user actions, and system-generated outputs.
FR-07: The system shall provide operational dashboards for throughput, failures, and business KPIs.
FR-08: The system shall support export or downstream handoff for approved outputs.
FR-09: The system shall handle partial integration failures with retry, queueing, and user-visible status.
FR-10: The system shall support audit review by exporting evidence for selected records.

## Non-Functional Requirements
NFR-01 Performance: Return eligibility and recommendations respond in <= 1.5 seconds P95.
NFR-02 Availability: 99.9% uptime during peak shopping windows.
NFR-03 Scalability: Support 50,000 return sessions per hour during January peak.
NFR-04 Security: Do not store payment card data; protect customer PII.
NFR-05 Recoverability: RPO <= 15 minutes and RTO <= 1 hour.
NFR-06 Maintainability: Return policies editable by operations with approval workflow.

## Constraints
- Team: Cross-functional product squad with 5-8 engineers, QA, security, and part-time domain experts.
- Timeline: MVP target in 11 weeks; production launch target in 15 weeks.
- Budget: Implementation must fit an annualized run-rate approved by the business sponsor.
- Technology: Prefer organization-approved cloud, identity, observability, and RAG tooling.
- Compliance: PCI scope avoidance, consumer refund laws, SOC 2 requirements must be traceable before launch.

## Risks
- RISK-01 (HIGH): Incorrect eligibility can create financial exposure - Mitigation: Centralize policy rules and test against historical orders.
- RISK-02 (MEDIUM): Inventory changes can make exchange recommendations stale - Mitigation: Reserve inventory only after shopper confirms.
- RISK-03 (MEDIUM): Carrier API outages can block labels - Mitigation: Fallback to delayed email label generation.

## Acceptance Criteria
- FR-01 through FR-04 pass integration tests using representative synthetic and historical sample data.
- All NFRs have automated or operational validation evidence before go-live.
- Security review confirms encryption, access control, audit logging, and data-retention behavior.
- Business owner signs off on pilot KPI dashboard and exception workflow.

## Success Criteria Post Launch
- Increase exchange selection from 12% to 22% of eligible returns.
- Reduce return-related support contacts by 30%.
- Issue return labels within 10 seconds for eligible orders.
