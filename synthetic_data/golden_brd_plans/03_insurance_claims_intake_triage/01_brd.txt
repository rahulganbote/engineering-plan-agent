# Business Requirements Document
# Project: AI-Assisted Property Claim Intake and Triage
# Domain: Insurance Claims | Synthetic Golden Dataset | Version: 1.0

## Background and Business Context
Digitize first notice of loss and triage property claims using document extraction, photo metadata, and policy coverage rules.

Current state: Manual FNOL processing averages 36 hours before adjuster assignment, creating leakage and poor customer experience after weather events.

Primary users: Policyholders, claims adjusters, claim supervisors.

## Objectives
1. Reduce average FNOL-to-assignment time from 36 hours to under 2 hours.
2. Auto-classify simple claims with >= 90% precision.
3. Prioritize severe claims and vulnerable customers for human review.
4. Maintain claim-file auditability for regulatory review.

## Stakeholders
- Product Owner: Claims Product Manager
- Technical Lead: Engineering Manager, Claims Platforms
- Business Sponsor: SVP Claims Transformation
- Primary Users: Policyholders, claims adjusters, claim supervisors
- Compliance / Security: NAIC model audit expectations, state claims handling rules, SOC 2 control owners
- External: Vendors and platform teams for Policy admin API, claims core system, document storage, geocoding/weather API, email/SMS notification

## Scope Boundaries
In Scope:
- Web and mobile FNOL intake with document and image upload.
- OCR and metadata extraction for loss descriptions, receipts, and photos.
- Coverage and severity triage rules.
- Claim assignment API integration.

Out of Scope:
- Automated settlement offers.
- Fraud investigation case management.
- Replacement of the policy administration system.

## Functional Requirements
FR-01: The system shall provide Web and mobile FNOL intake with document and image upload.
FR-02: The system shall integrate with Policy admin API, claims core system, document storage, geocoding/weather API, email/SMS notification.
FR-03: The system shall manage data assets including Policy coverage, FNOL forms, uploaded photos, loss location, weather-event data, adjuster queues.
FR-04: The system shall enforce compliance controls for NAIC model audit expectations, state claims handling rules, SOC 2.
FR-05: The system shall expose role-based workflows for primary users.
FR-06: The system shall record decision provenance, user actions, and system-generated outputs.
FR-07: The system shall provide operational dashboards for throughput, failures, and business KPIs.
FR-08: The system shall support export or downstream handoff for approved outputs.
FR-09: The system shall handle partial integration failures with retry, queueing, and user-visible status.
FR-10: The system shall support audit review by exporting evidence for selected records.

## Non-Functional Requirements
NFR-01 Performance: Submit FNOL and receive triage result in <= 5 seconds P95 excluding upload time.
NFR-02 Availability: 99.9% uptime and disaster-event surge mode within 30 minutes.
NFR-03 Scalability: Handle 10x normal claim volume during catastrophe events.
NFR-04 Security: Encrypt documents and restrict claim file access by role and region.
NFR-05 Recoverability: RPO <= 15 minutes and RTO <= 2 hours.
NFR-06 Observability: Expose queue depth, OCR confidence, and triage decision traces.

## Constraints
- Team: Cross-functional product squad with 5-8 engineers, QA, security, and part-time domain experts.
- Timeline: MVP target in 16 weeks; production launch target in 20 weeks.
- Budget: Implementation must fit an annualized run-rate approved by the business sponsor.
- Technology: Prefer organization-approved cloud, identity, observability, and RAG tooling.
- Compliance: NAIC model audit expectations, state claims handling rules, SOC 2 requirements must be traceable before launch.

## Risks
- RISK-01 (HIGH): OCR quality may be poor for damaged documents - Mitigation: Use confidence thresholds and human review fallback.
- RISK-02 (HIGH): Catastrophe surge can overload assignment queues - Mitigation: Implement backpressure and priority routing.
- RISK-03 (MEDIUM): Coverage rules vary by state - Mitigation: Externalize state-specific rules and legal review.

## Acceptance Criteria
- FR-01 through FR-04 pass integration tests using representative synthetic and historical sample data.
- All NFRs have automated or operational validation evidence before go-live.
- Security review confirms encryption, access control, audit logging, and data-retention behavior.
- Business owner signs off on pilot KPI dashboard and exception workflow.

## Success Criteria Post Launch
- Reduce average FNOL-to-assignment time from 36 hours to under 2 hours.
- Auto-classify simple claims with >= 90% precision.
- Prioritize severe claims and vulnerable customers for human review.
