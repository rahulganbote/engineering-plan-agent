## Pattern Selection
pattern: Modular monolith with async ingestion
pattern_justification: |
  A six-person product team can ship faster with one deployable while using async workers for data ingestion; microservices were rejected until usage and team boundaries justify them. Team size is assumed to be 5-8 engineers, which is enough for clear component ownership without creating unnecessary platform overhead. The primary NFR driver is Generate forecasts for a customer with 24 months of history in <= 8 seconds P95. The architecture respects compliance constraints for GLBA, SOC 2, consent-management policy.
