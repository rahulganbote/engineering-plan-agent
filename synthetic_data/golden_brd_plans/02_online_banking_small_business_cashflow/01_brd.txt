# Business Requirements Document
# Project: Small Business Cash-Flow Forecasting Portal
# Domain: Online Banking | Synthetic Golden Dataset | Version: 1.0

## Background and Business Context
Provide small-business customers with short-term cash-flow forecasts grounded in account history, invoices, and scheduled payments.

Current state: Business customers rely on manual spreadsheets and call relationship managers for liquidity guidance, increasing support cost and churn risk.

Primary users: Small-business owners, relationship managers, treasury support.

## Objectives
1. Deliver 13-week cash-flow forecasts with >= 85% directional accuracy for pilot customers.
2. Reduce relationship-manager forecast-preparation time from 2 hours to 15 minutes.
3. Surface explainable drivers behind projected shortfalls.
4. Support exportable board-ready forecast summaries.

## Stakeholders
- Product Owner: Product Owner, Business Digital Banking
- Technical Lead: Principal Engineer, Financial Insights
- Business Sponsor: Head of Small Business Banking
- Primary Users: Small-business owners, relationship managers, treasury support
- Compliance / Security: GLBA, SOC 2, consent-management policy control owners
- External: Vendors and platform teams for Open banking aggregation API, core deposit API, invoice import, CRM, PDF export service

## Scope Boundaries
In Scope:
- Consent-based ingestion of account transactions, invoices, and scheduled bill payments.
- Forecast engine with recurring transaction detection and scenario adjustments.
- Relationship-manager review workflow.
- PDF and CSV forecast exports.

Out of Scope:
- Loan origination decisions.
- Automated cash transfers.
- External accounting package write-back.

## Functional Requirements
FR-01: The system shall provide Consent-based ingestion of account transactions, invoices, and scheduled bill payments.
FR-02: The system shall integrate with Open banking aggregation API, core deposit API, invoice import, CRM, PDF export service.
FR-03: The system shall manage data assets including Account balances, transaction history, invoice due dates, scheduled payments, user-entered scenarios.
FR-04: The system shall enforce compliance controls for GLBA, SOC 2, consent-management policy.
FR-05: The system shall expose role-based workflows for primary users.
FR-06: The system shall record decision provenance, user actions, and system-generated outputs.
FR-07: The system shall provide operational dashboards for throughput, failures, and business KPIs.
FR-08: The system shall support export or downstream handoff for approved outputs.
FR-09: The system shall handle partial integration failures with retry, queueing, and user-visible status.
FR-10: The system shall support audit review by exporting evidence for selected records.

## Non-Functional Requirements
NFR-01 Performance: Generate forecasts for a customer with 24 months of history in <= 8 seconds P95.
NFR-02 Availability: 99.9% uptime during business banking hours.
NFR-03 Scalability: Support 25,000 business customers and 500 relationship managers.
NFR-04 Security: Tokenize account identifiers and isolate customer data by tenant.
NFR-05 Recoverability: RPO <= 15 minutes and RTO <= 1 hour.
NFR-06 Maintainability: Forecast feature weights configurable without deployment.

## Constraints
- Team: Cross-functional product squad with 5-8 engineers, QA, security, and part-time domain experts.
- Timeline: MVP target in 12 weeks; production launch target in 16 weeks.
- Budget: Implementation must fit an annualized run-rate approved by the business sponsor.
- Technology: Prefer organization-approved cloud, identity, observability, and RAG tooling.
- Compliance: GLBA, SOC 2, consent-management policy requirements must be traceable before launch.

## Risks
- RISK-01 (HIGH): Forecast accuracy may vary by industry seasonality - Mitigation: Segment pilot by industry and measure accuracy separately.
- RISK-02 (MEDIUM): Customer consent revocation can create partial data gaps - Mitigation: Design forecast confidence bands and data freshness warnings.
- RISK-03 (MEDIUM): PDF exports may be treated as financial advice - Mitigation: Legal review and disclaimers in every export.

## Acceptance Criteria
- FR-01 through FR-04 pass integration tests using representative synthetic and historical sample data.
- All NFRs have automated or operational validation evidence before go-live.
- Security review confirms encryption, access control, audit logging, and data-retention behavior.
- Business owner signs off on pilot KPI dashboard and exception workflow.

## Success Criteria Post Launch
- Deliver 13-week cash-flow forecasts with >= 85% directional accuracy for pilot customers.
- Reduce relationship-manager forecast-preparation time from 2 hours to 15 minutes.
- Surface explainable drivers behind projected shortfalls.
