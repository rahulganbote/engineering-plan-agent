# Business Requirements Document
# Project: Clinical Trial Recruitment Matching Platform
# Domain: Pharmaceutical | Synthetic Golden Dataset | Version: 1.0

## Background and Business Context
Match eligible patients to clinical trials using protocol criteria, EHR extracts, and consent-aware outreach workflows.

Current state: Trial enrollment delays average 11 weeks because coordinators manually screen charts against complex inclusion and exclusion criteria.

Primary users: Clinical research coordinators, investigators, patient navigators.

## Objectives
1. Reduce chart-screening effort by 50% for pilot oncology trials.
2. Identify candidate patients with >= 85% recall against coordinator-reviewed baselines.
3. Respect consent and site-specific recruitment rules.
4. Produce inspection-ready evidence for match decisions.

## Stakeholders
- Product Owner: Clinical Trial Digital Product Owner
- Technical Lead: Engineering Lead, Clinical Systems
- Business Sponsor: VP Clinical Operations
- Primary Users: Clinical research coordinators, investigators, patient navigators
- Compliance / Security: HIPAA, GxP validation expectations, 21 CFR Part 11, IRB recruitment rules control owners
- External: Vendors and platform teams for FHIR server, CTMS, eConsent system, identity provider, secure email

## Scope Boundaries
In Scope:
- Protocol criteria ingestion and structured rule extraction.
- EHR cohort matching from FHIR resources and lab data.
- Coordinator review worklist with match rationale.
- Consent-aware outreach handoff.

Out of Scope:
- Patient diagnosis or treatment recommendations.
- Direct patient messaging without coordinator approval.
- Clinical trial management system replacement.

## Functional Requirements
FR-01: The system shall provide Protocol criteria ingestion and structured rule extraction.
FR-02: The system shall integrate with FHIR server, CTMS, eConsent system, identity provider, secure email.
FR-03: The system shall manage data assets including FHIR Conditions, Observations, Medications, demographics, consent status, protocol eligibility criteria.
FR-04: The system shall enforce compliance controls for HIPAA, GxP validation expectations, 21 CFR Part 11, IRB recruitment rules.
FR-05: The system shall expose role-based workflows for primary users.
FR-06: The system shall record decision provenance, user actions, and system-generated outputs.
FR-07: The system shall provide operational dashboards for throughput, failures, and business KPIs.
FR-08: The system shall support export or downstream handoff for approved outputs.
FR-09: The system shall handle partial integration failures with retry, queueing, and user-visible status.
FR-10: The system shall support audit review by exporting evidence for selected records.

## Non-Functional Requirements
NFR-01 Performance: Refresh candidate lists for a trial with 100,000 patients in <= 2 hours.
NFR-02 Availability: 99.5% uptime for coordinator worklists.
NFR-03 Scalability: Support 40 active trials and 2 million patient records.
NFR-04 Security: Protect PHI with role-based access and full audit logs.
NFR-05 Recoverability: RPO <= 30 minutes and RTO <= 4 hours.
NFR-06 Explainability: Every match must cite criteria and patient facts used.

## Constraints
- Team: Cross-functional product squad with 5-8 engineers, QA, security, and part-time domain experts.
- Timeline: MVP target in 18 weeks; production launch target in 22 weeks.
- Budget: Implementation must fit an annualized run-rate approved by the business sponsor.
- Technology: Prefer organization-approved cloud, identity, observability, and RAG tooling.
- Compliance: HIPAA, GxP validation expectations, 21 CFR Part 11, IRB recruitment rules requirements must be traceable before launch.

## Risks
- RISK-01 (HIGH): LLM criteria extraction could miss exclusions - Mitigation: Require human approval of extracted criteria and locked versions.
- RISK-02 (HIGH): EHR data quality can reduce recall - Mitigation: Profile missingness and flag low-confidence matches.
- RISK-03 (MEDIUM): Part 11 expectations may expand validation scope - Mitigation: Engage QA validation lead in Sprint 1.

## Acceptance Criteria
- FR-01 through FR-04 pass integration tests using representative synthetic and historical sample data.
- All NFRs have automated or operational validation evidence before go-live.
- Security review confirms encryption, access control, audit logging, and data-retention behavior.
- Business owner signs off on pilot KPI dashboard and exception workflow.

## Success Criteria Post Launch
- Reduce chart-screening effort by 50% for pilot oncology trials.
- Identify candidate patients with >= 85% recall against coordinator-reviewed baselines.
- Respect consent and site-specific recruitment rules.
