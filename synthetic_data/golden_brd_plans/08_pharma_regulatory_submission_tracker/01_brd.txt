# Business Requirements Document
# Project: Regulatory Submission Readiness Tracker
# Domain: Pharmaceutical | Synthetic Golden Dataset | Version: 1.0

## Background and Business Context
Track module readiness, document quality gates, and cross-functional approvals for eCTD regulatory submissions.

Current state: Submission teams rely on spreadsheets, causing duplicate document work, missed dependencies, and late publishing defects.

Primary users: Regulatory affairs managers, publishing specialists, quality reviewers.

## Objectives
1. Provide real-time readiness status for every eCTD module.
2. Reduce late-cycle publishing defects by 40%.
3. Automate dependency alerts for missing authoring, QC, and approval tasks.
4. Maintain Part 11-compliant audit trails for readiness changes.

## Stakeholders
- Product Owner: Regulatory Systems Product Owner
- Technical Lead: Solution Architect, Regulatory Platforms
- Business Sponsor: Head of Regulatory Affairs
- Primary Users: Regulatory affairs managers, publishing specialists, quality reviewers
- Compliance / Security: 21 CFR Part 11, GxP validation, ICH eCTD conventions control owners
- External: Vendors and platform teams for Veeva Vault or equivalent DMS, identity provider, email/Teams alerts, regulatory archive

## Scope Boundaries
In Scope:
- Submission plan setup by product, market, and sequence.
- Document readiness ingestion from document management system.
- Workflow gates for authoring, QC, regulatory review, and publishing.
- Dashboards and alerts for dependency risks.

Out of Scope:
- Actual eCTD publishing/rendering engine.
- Authoring document templates.
- Health authority gateway submission.

## Functional Requirements
FR-01: The system shall provide Submission plan setup by product, market, and sequence.
FR-02: The system shall integrate with Veeva Vault or equivalent DMS, identity provider, email/Teams alerts, regulatory archive.
FR-03: The system shall manage data assets including Submission plans, document metadata, QC status, approval evidence, market-specific dependencies.
FR-04: The system shall enforce compliance controls for 21 CFR Part 11, GxP validation, ICH eCTD conventions.
FR-05: The system shall expose role-based workflows for primary users.
FR-06: The system shall record decision provenance, user actions, and system-generated outputs.
FR-07: The system shall provide operational dashboards for throughput, failures, and business KPIs.
FR-08: The system shall support export or downstream handoff for approved outputs.
FR-09: The system shall handle partial integration failures with retry, queueing, and user-visible status.
FR-10: The system shall support audit review by exporting evidence for selected records.

## Non-Functional Requirements
NFR-01 Performance: Dashboard loads readiness for 5,000 documents in <= 3 seconds P95.
NFR-02 Availability: 99.7% uptime during business hours across regions.
NFR-03 Scalability: Support 300 active submissions and 50,000 tracked documents.
NFR-04 Security: Enforce SSO, e-signature evidence, and immutable audit logs.
NFR-05 Recoverability: RPO <= 15 minutes and RTO <= 2 hours.
NFR-06 Maintainability: Market-specific dependency rules configurable by regulatory operations.

## Constraints
- Team: Cross-functional product squad with 5-8 engineers, QA, security, and part-time domain experts.
- Timeline: MVP target in 15 weeks; production launch target in 19 weeks.
- Budget: Implementation must fit an annualized run-rate approved by the business sponsor.
- Technology: Prefer organization-approved cloud, identity, observability, and RAG tooling.
- Compliance: 21 CFR Part 11, GxP validation, ICH eCTD conventions requirements must be traceable before launch.

## Risks
- RISK-01 (HIGH): DMS metadata quality may be inconsistent - Mitigation: Profile metadata and build reconciliation reports.
- RISK-02 (MEDIUM): Part 11 validation adds timeline overhead - Mitigation: Plan validation deliverables and traceability from Sprint 1.
- RISK-03 (MEDIUM): Alert fatigue can reduce adoption - Mitigation: Tune alerts by critical path and recipient role.

## Acceptance Criteria
- FR-01 through FR-04 pass integration tests using representative synthetic and historical sample data.
- All NFRs have automated or operational validation evidence before go-live.
- Security review confirms encryption, access control, audit logging, and data-retention behavior.
- Business owner signs off on pilot KPI dashboard and exception workflow.

## Success Criteria Post Launch
- Provide real-time readiness status for every eCTD module.
- Reduce late-cycle publishing defects by 40%.
- Automate dependency alerts for missing authoring, QC, and approval tasks.
