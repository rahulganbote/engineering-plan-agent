# Business Requirements Document
# Project: Employee Directory Mobile App
# Domain: Internal Tools | Complexity: Simple

## Background
The company of 800 employees currently uses a static intranet page for the employee
directory. It is updated manually, often out of date, and not accessible on mobile.
HR has requested a mobile-friendly employee directory app.

## Objectives
1. Provide real-time employee directory accessible on iOS and Android
2. Enable employees to update their own profiles without IT involvement
3. Support search by name, department, location, and skill

## Functional Requirements
FR-01: Employees can search directory by name, title, department, and location
FR-02: Each employee profile shows photo, title, team, manager, email, and Slack handle
FR-03: Employees can update their own photo, bio, and skills
FR-04: Managers can update team membership for their direct reports
FR-05: Directory syncs with Okta SSO for login and deprovisioning

## Non-Functional Requirements
NFR-01: App loads directory search results in < 2 seconds
NFR-02: Available on iOS 15+ and Android 11+
NFR-03: 99.5% availability during business hours

## Constraints
- Team: 2 engineers, 8 weeks, no new infrastructure budget
- Must integrate with Okta (existing SSO provider)
- No PII stored outside existing AWS us-east-1 region

## Risks
- RISK-01 (LOW): Okta API rate limits during peak usage
- RISK-02 (LOW): Employee photo upload size and storage costs
