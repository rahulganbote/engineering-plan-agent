# Business Requirements Document
# Project: Coffee Shop Mobile Ordering App
# Domain: Consumer Retail | Complexity: Simple

## Background
Corner Café has one location and a line out the door every morning. Customers
often skip the shop entirely because the wait is too long. The owner wants a
mobile app so customers can order and pay ahead, then just walk in and grab
their drink.

## Objectives
1. Let customers order and pay from their phone before arriving
2. Reduce average in-store wait time during the morning rush
3. Build repeat visits with a simple loyalty rewards program

## Functional Requirements
FR-01: Customers can browse the menu and customize drinks (size, milk, shots)
FR-02: Customers can place an order and pay with a saved card or Apple Pay
FR-03: Customers receive a push notification when their order is ready
FR-04: Customers earn 1 point per dollar spent; 100 points = free drink
FR-05: Baristas see incoming orders on a tablet in pickup order

## Non-Functional Requirements
NFR-01: Order confirmation screen loads in < 2 seconds
NFR-02: Available on iOS 15+ and Android 11+
NFR-03: 99.5% uptime during store hours (6am-7pm)

## Constraints
- Team: 2 engineers, 8 weeks, no new infrastructure budget
- Must integrate with Square (existing point-of-sale system)
- Card payment data must never touch our own servers (PCI compliance)

## Risks
- RISK-01 (LOW): Square API rate limits during morning rush
- RISK-02 (LOW): Customers arriving before order is actually ready
