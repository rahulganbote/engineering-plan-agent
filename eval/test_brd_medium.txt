# Business Requirements Document
# Project: Customer Analytics & Reporting Platform
# Domain: SaaS / Data Platform | Complexity: Medium | Version: 1.0

## Background and Business Context
The organization's product and growth teams currently rely on manual exports
from five disconnected tools (Mixpanel, Salesforce, Zendesk, Stripe, and
internal PostgreSQL databases) to assemble weekly business reports. This process
takes 2-3 days per analyst per week and produces inconsistent results due to
differing data definitions across teams. A unified analytics platform is needed
to reduce reporting time, improve data consistency, and enable self-serve
analytics for non-technical stakeholders.

## Objectives
1. Reduce weekly reporting cycle from 3 days to under 4 hours
2. Provide a single source of truth for all customer and revenue metrics
3. Enable non-technical users (PMs, CS, Sales) to build their own reports
4. Support real-time dashboards refreshing within 15 minutes of source data change
5. Achieve SOC 2 Type II compliance for the analytics layer within 9 months

## Stakeholders
- Product Owner: Head of Product Analytics
- Engineering Lead: Senior Data Engineer
- Business Sponsors: VP Product, VP Revenue Operations
- Primary Users: Product Managers (12), Customer Success (8), Sales Ops (5)
- Compliance: InfoSec team
- External: Snowflake (vendor), dbt Labs (tooling)

## Functional Requirements
FR-01: System shall ingest data from Mixpanel, Salesforce, Zendesk, Stripe, and PostgreSQL
FR-02: System shall provide a self-serve report builder with drag-and-drop interface
FR-03: System shall support scheduled reports delivered via email or Slack
FR-04: System shall maintain a centralized metric definitions catalog
FR-05: System shall support row-level security so users only see permitted data
FR-06: System shall provide pre-built dashboards for Product, Revenue, and CS teams
FR-07: System shall support data export in CSV, Excel, and PDF formats
FR-08: System shall provide audit logs of all data access and report creation
FR-09: System shall alert on anomalous metric movements (>2 standard deviations)
FR-10: System shall support metric versioning - old reports must remain consistent

## Non-Functional Requirements
NFR-01 Performance: Dashboard load time < 5 seconds for standard queries
NFR-02 Freshness: Data refreshed within 15 minutes of source system change
NFR-03 Availability: 99.5% uptime during business hours (8am-8pm local)
NFR-04 Scalability: Support 50 concurrent users with < 8 second degradation
NFR-05 Security: SOC 2 Type II · row-level security · all data encrypted at rest
NFR-06 Usability: Non-technical users able to build first report in < 30 minutes

## Constraints
- Engineering team: 3 data engineers, 1 analytics engineer, 0.5 DevOps
- Timeline: MVP in 12 weeks, full feature set in 20 weeks
- Budget: $180,000 annual infrastructure + tooling
- Must use existing Snowflake contract (enterprise license already paid)
- dbt is the approved transformation layer - no alternatives
- Cannot replace Salesforce or Stripe - integration only, no migration
- Security review required before any production deployment

## Risks
- RISK-01 (HIGH): Mixpanel API rate limits may slow initial historical data load (60-90 days)
- RISK-02 (HIGH): Inconsistent data definitions across teams will require governance process
- RISK-03 (MEDIUM): Row-level security complexity in Snowflake may extend timeline
- RISK-04 (MEDIUM): Non-technical user adoption requires training investment
- RISK-05 (LOW): dbt version upgrades may break existing transformation models

## Success Criteria
- Reporting cycle reduced from 3 days to < 4 hours within 60 days of launch
- 80% of target users actively using platform within 90 days of launch
- Zero P1 data accuracy incidents in first 6 months
- SOC 2 Type II certification achieved within 9 months
