## Background and Business Context
The company needs a simple tool for employees to log their daily working hours.
Currently this is done on paper timesheets which are then manually entered into
a spreadsheet by the HR assistant. The process takes 2 hours every Friday.

## Objectives
1. Allow employees to log daily start and end times digitally
2. Generate a weekly CSV report of hours worked per employee
3. Allow managers to approve or reject submitted timesheets

## Stakeholders
- Product Owner: HR Manager
- Primary Users: 45 employees, 6 managers
- Technical Lead: Junior Developer

## Functional Requirements
FR-01: Employees shall log start time, end time, and a short task description each day
FR-02: System shall calculate total hours worked per day automatically
FR-03: System shall generate a weekly CSV report showing hours per employee per day
FR-04: Managers shall review and approve or reject timesheets submitted by their reports
FR-05: Employees shall receive a confirmation when their timesheet is approved

## Non-Functional Requirements
NFR-01 Availability: System available during business hours 8am-6pm Monday to Friday
NFR-02 Performance: Page loads in under 3 seconds on standard company laptops

## Constraints
- Team: 1 junior developer, 0.5 QA
- Timeline: 4 weeks
- Budget: $0 infrastructure - must use free tier services only
- No external integrations permitted - standalone system only

## Risks
- RISK-01 (LOW): Junior developer may underestimate complexity of approval workflow
  Mitigation: Daily check-in with tech lead during Week 3
